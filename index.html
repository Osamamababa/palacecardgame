Palace

  • How To Play

    Official Rules of Palace

    • Card Hierarchy: Standard ranking applies from 3 (lowest) up to Ace (highest), with specific exceptions for power cards.
    • Play Requirements: To successfully discard, a played card must be equal to or higher in rank than the top card of the discard pile. If a player cannot make a legal play, they must forfeit their turn and pick up the entire discard pile into their hand.
    • Power Cards (2s and 10s):
      • Twos (2): Act as wild cards that can be played on any rank. Playing a 2 resets hierarchy of the discard pile and grants the same player an immediate bonus turn to play any card of their choosing.
      • Tens (10): Share the hierarchy resetting capability of 2s, but additionally clear the discard pile entirely from the game, permanently removing all discarded cards from the active match.
    • Four-of-a-Kind Bonus: If four cards of identical rank are played consecutively onto the discard pile, the pile is cleared identically to a 10, and the player responsible for placing the fourth card earns an immediate bonus turn.
    • Hand Replenishment: Provided the stock deck contains cards, all players are required to maintain a minimum hand size of three cards at the conclusion of their turn.
    • Consecutive Matching Plays: During a player's turn (Player A), until the subsequent player (Player B) completes a turn, Player A retains the right to play any additional cards matching the rank of their previous play.
    • Palace Progression:
      • Players may only draw from and play their Face-Up Palace Cards once their hand is completely empty.
      • Players may only access and play their Face-Down Palace Cards once both their hand and face-up cards are entirely depleted.
      • Face-up and face-down palace cards must be played strictly one at a time, with the exception of 2s and 10s.
